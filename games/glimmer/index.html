Glimmer Guard

Rogue glow-drones have taken over the night greenhouse. Tag them with your light blaster before they drain your shield.

Tip: turn your phone sideways for a wider view. Both orientations work.

Controls & settings

Touch: left thumb moves, drag right side to look, gold button fires, small button reloads.
Keyboard: WASD to move, mouse to look (click to lock), click to fire, R reload, Esc pause.
